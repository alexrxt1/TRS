Complete End-To-End Roof Glazing Solutions

Our Specialized Rooflight Services

We provide a complete, end-to-end rooflight service, combining expert technical knowledge with practical delivery across commercial, public sector, and high-end residential properties.

Complete Technical Expertise & Delivery

Our offering includes the supply and installation of new rooflights, like-for-like replacements and performance upgrades, refurbishment, repairs, and ongoing servicing and maintenance. We carry out detailed rooflight surveys, inspections, leak investigations and condition reports to assess existing assets and inform future works.

From straightforward maintenance to complex or bespoke rooflight solutions, we support both new-build and refurbishment projects, ensuring safe, compliant and high-performing roof glazing at every stage.

01 / Core Capability

Supply & Installation

We supply and install top-tier glass and polycarbonate rooflight systems tailored to exact architectural specifications.

Inquire about Installation →

New Rooflight Systems

Flat glass units, roof lanterns, continuous vaulted glazing, walk-on glass, and modular skylights installed to precision tolerances.

Like-for-Like Replacements

Seamless replacement of aged, discolored, or defective roof glazing without requiring structural alterations to upstands.

Thermal & Solar Upgrades

High-performance double/triple glazing with low-E coatings, solar control tints, argon fill, and easy-clean self-cleaning technology.

Full Structural Compliance

Guaranteed compliance with UK Building Regulations, CWCT standards, wind/snow loads, and non-fragility guidelines.

02 / Diagnostic Excellence

Surveys & Inspections

Detailed site condition audits, leak diagnostics, and asset inspections to determine the root cause of issues and provide actionable technical reports.

Book a Rooflight Survey →

Leak Investigations

Pinpoint water ingress channels using electronic leak detection, water testing, thermal imaging, and pressure diagnostics.

Condition & Asset Reports

Comprehensive photographic and technical reports rating remaining lifespan, safety risks, and remedial priority schedules.

Structural & Seal Audits

Evaluating structural integrity, silicone degradation, frame corrosion, gasket integrity, and flashing interfaces.

Pre-Purchase Inspections

Independent expert assessment for property buyers, surveying firms, facility managers, and insurance claims.

03 / Target Remediation

Refurbishment & Repairs

Targeted restoration programs that breathe new life into existing roof glazing, restoring watertight integrity and thermal efficiency at a fraction of full replacement cost.

Request Repair Quote →

Structural Glass Pane Replacement

Safe extraction and replacement of cracked, blown, fogged, or failed double-glazed units using specialist craneage or manual suction riggers.

Gasket & Seal Renewal

Full removal of perished silicone seals, installation of commercial structural weatherproofing sealant, and UV-resistant EPDM gaskets.

Flashing & Upstand Integration

Repairing single-ply, felt, or lead flashing connections around curbs and upstands to eliminate perimeter water ingress.

Condensation Resolution

Installing thermal breaks, trickle ventilation, and improved perimeter insulation to eradicate cold bridging and internal dripping.

04 / Maintenance & Custom Engineering

Servicing & Bespoke Engineering

Preventative maintenance contracts for ongoing compliance and bespoke engineered glazing solutions for complex architectural concepts.

Discuss Custom Project →

Planned Preventative Maintenance

Annual or bi-annual inspection visits, drainage channel clearing, hardware lubrication, and proactive seal checks to preserve warranties.

Actuator & AOV Servicing

Testing and recalibrating electric chain actuators, smoke vent controls (AOV), rain sensors, and automated opening mechanism switches.

Bespoke Architectural Glazing

Custom-engineered multi-pitch lanterns, complex multi-section structural glass, non-standard shapes, and oversized skylights.

Heritage & Listed Building Glazing

Sensitively designed replacement units for period structures, traditional timber cappings, and conservation-approved profiles.
